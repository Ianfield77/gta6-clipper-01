# gta6-clipper-01 — GTA 6 grandma has two hammers

A 12 second vertical Short for the Make.fun bounty
"Clip GTA 6 for the most YouTube views" (gta6-clipper-01, $500, 20 places
by views, closes Monday 23 Nov 2026 08:00 WIB).

## The clip

`clip.mp4` — 1080x1920 (9:16), 12 seconds, H.264 + AAC.

- 0.0-1.9s: the moment. A grandma in a floral nightgown and slippers,
  a hammer in each hand, staring deadpan into the camera, from Grand
  Theft Auto VI Trailer 1 (Rockstar Games), trailer time 57.4-59.3s.
  One continuous shot, not a montage. The in-universe caption reads
  "Neighborhood watch don't play around in Hamlet". Hook text "GRANDMA
  HAS TWO HAMMERS" on screen from the first frame, in a black bar.
- 1.9-12.0s: the pitch. Magenta end card with the make.fun ghost and
  the lines "MAKE FUNNY CLIPS LIKE THIS WITH AI" and "AND EARN MONEY
  ON MAKE.FUN", then the bounty address
  "make.fun/bounty/gta6-clipper-01". Spoken by a voiceover: "Make funny
  clips like this with AI, and earn money on make.fun. The bounty is on
  make.fun."

Why this moment: the first clip (bank robbery, Trailer 2) was intense
but generic action. This one is absurd and unique: nobody expects the
neighborhood watch to be a grandma dual-wielding hammers, and the joke
lands in the first second, which is everything on a quiet channel. The
source is already a vertical phone video inside the trailer, so she
fills the whole 9:16 frame instead of sitting small in a landscape
shot. Runner-ups found while scanning: the alligator at the pool party
(Trailer 1, ~42.5s, too small in frame) and the bench press on the
beach (Trailer 2, ~39s, one static shot with no punchline). The channel
is @Oktafianaldy, last video 4 years ago, so the clip is built to be
as punchy as a 12 second Short can be.
